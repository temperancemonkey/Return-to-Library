Return to Library was formed in our school library as emerging designers with a shared fascination for experimental publishing and cool books. Individually, our members have tabled at fairs including the Offset Art Book Fair, the Newark Zine Fest, and the Spitting Image Art Book Fair. Coming together, we formed Return to Library as a creative platform to learn, collaborate, and design as a collective.
